SOFTWARE & HARDWARE PORTFOLIO

Engineered & Programmed in Aotearoa

From the foundational SDR data logger software in the 1980s to real-time machine guidance, precision agriculture, hydrographic dredging, and seismic timber engineering, New Zealand has been the software engine room of Trimble’s global Field Solutions and Heavy Civil divisions.

NEW ZEALAND INVESTMENT HISTORY

Trimble's New Zealand Acquisitions Portfolio

Starting with Charlie Trimble's historic 1991 acquisition of Datacom Software Research in Christchurch, Trimble continued to invest across New Zealand's technology hubs in Auckland, Rotorua, and Wellington.

CRISIS & REBUILDING RESILIENCE

The Christchurch Crucible & The 2014 Pres-Lam Rebuild

In 2011, Christchurch experienced an unprecedented double blow: the catastrophic February 22 earthquake, followed three months later by a massive fire that destroyed Building One at 11 Birmingham Drive. Rather than retreating, Trimble International reaffirmed its dedication to Christchurch, embarking on a $23 million timber engineering marvel that set a global benchmark for low-damage earthquake resilience.

February 2011 Quake Disruption

The M6.3 earthquake severely damaged Christchurch infrastructure. Under Managing Director Corinne Haines, Trimble teams activated cloud recovery and remote sprint workflows, keeping global survey software releases and dealer deliveries completely on schedule with zero loss of client data.

Visual on Wall: Damaged Canterbury city center & seismic monitoring telemetry
May 2011 Fire Building One

An accidental electrical fire completely consumed Building One. Trimble leadership flew to NZ and publicly pledged to keep Christchurch as Trimble's primary R&D home, quickly establishing temporary portacabin offices on site to keep developers coding.

Visual on Wall: Night photo of flames engulfing Building One at Birmingham Drive
Pres-Lam Rocking Timber World First

Designed with WSP/Opus and City Care, the new 6,000 m² headquarters opened in April 2014. It was the first commercial building in the world combining post-tensioned laminated veneer lumber (LVL) rocking frames, rocking shear walls, and timber-concrete composite floors designed to rock and self-center during quakes.

Visual on Wall: Twilight photo of glowing glass-timber facade with Corinne Haines' quote
Living Laboratory Seismic Sensors

The building is outfitted with embedded Trimble seismic accelerometers and displacement sensors, providing structural engineering researchers at the University of Canterbury real-time telemetry on low-damage timber performance.
