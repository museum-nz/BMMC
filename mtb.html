⚖️ Official Trail Data Advisory

Reference Purposes Only: Geospatial trail alignments, classifications, grades, and distance metrics presented in this application are compiled for general reference and informational guidance only, based on records available at the time of publication.

Not for Critical Navigation: Users must not rely solely upon this digital map for route-finding, backcountry navigation, or safety-critical decisions. Trail geometry may deviate from actual ground truth due to slips, forestry works, natural realignment, or environmental changes.

Verify Current Status: Track conditions, legal access permissions, seasonal closures, and safety warnings are subject to frequent alteration without notice. Always verify current track status and regional alerts directly with the responsible land manager—including the Department of Conservation (DOC), Christchurch City Council (CCC), Christchurch Adventure Park (CAP), New Zealand Cycle Trails (Ngā Haerenga), or local trail trusts—prior to departure.
